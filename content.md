##### Grass Chomper
A 3” long grasshopper with sharp teeth capable of chewing through flesh and rooty vegetation. Will aggressively swarm undead.

**AC** 19* **HP** 4 **ATK** 1 Bite -1 (1d4) **MV** Near (ground) Close (fly) **S** -1 **D** +4 **C** +0 **I** -3 **W** -2 **Ch** -3 **AL** L **LV** 1

**Swarming.** When swarming in groups of 5+ their AC drops to 14. When attacking make 1 attack role for the group and any damage rolls forward. Bite now attacks with +2 (1d10).
- 5+ swarm: 2 bite +2 (1d4)
- 10+ swarm: 3 bite +3 (1d4)
- 15+ swarm: 4 bite +4 (1d4)
- 20+ swarm: 5 bite +5 (1d4)
